Every week, a small bakery in our town throws away about forty loaves of bread. Not because the bread is bad, but because nobody knew it was there.

We built Crumb. At six p.m., the bakery snaps one photo of what is left. Crumb turns that photo into a listing, and people within one kilometre get a ping: four loaves, half price, pick up before seven.

Last month we ran this with twelve bakeries. They sold seventy percent of their leftovers and earned an extra three hundred euros each.

Bakers pay nothing up front. We take ten percent of each sale.

We are raising two hundred thousand euros to reach five hundred shops by next summer. If you have ever watched good food go in the bin, you already understand Crumb.
